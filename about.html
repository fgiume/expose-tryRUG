About us

Our story

Why EXPOSE exists

Sanctions are widely used in international politics, yet we know surprisingly little about the different ways they are evaded. Research has often focused on individual countries or particular methods, making it difficult to compare what happens across sanctions regimes.

Reports by United Nations Panels of Experts offer an unusually detailed window into this issue. They document suspected violations, transactions and methods that would otherwise be difficult to observe. But this evidence is spread across many lengthy reports and is not always easy to compare.

EXPOSE brings this material together and develops a common vocabulary for studying sanctions evasion. By identifying restrictions, strategies and specific tactics in the reported evidence, we aim to make patterns easier to investigate across countries and over time.

These reports are a valuable starting point, not a complete account of every violation. EXPOSE is an ongoing research effort to make the available evidence more accessible and useful for understanding how sanctions work in practice.

What we do

Three things we aim for

Make evidence findable

One place to search the evasion methods that Panel reports describe, instead of scanning each report separately.

Compare across regimes

See whether techniques seen with one sanctions regime also appear in others, such as ships going dark or front companies.

Support better enforcement

Give compliance staff, journalists and policymakers indicators they can look for in practice.
